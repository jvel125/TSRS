What We Do

Capabilities &
Services

TSRS delivers end-to-end rail component solutions, from sourcing and fabrication to certified reconditioning, all under one roof in Dayton, Texas.

01

Material Sourcing

Currently hold AAR Voluntary (VOL) code for Distribution of Components & Parts. We identify the right components at the right price, domestically and internationally.

02

Component Fabrication

Major and minor rail subassemblies from global suppliers meeting AAR quality standards. Custom welding and fabrication to exact customer specifications.

03

Truck Bolster & Side Frame Reconditioning

Full reconditioning meeting AAR standards, restoring gibs, liners, wear plates, and bowls. Brings worn components back to spec, extending asset life and reducing capital spend.

04

Truck Mounted Air Brake Rebuilding

Clean, lube, and testing of TMB pistons and cylinders (TMX, TRIAX, Ellcon National). Full documentation and traceability to AAR and FRA standards.

05

Quality / Consulting Compliance

QA/Operation Procedure creation, SOP, Process audits, Mechanical Audits, Corrective and Preventive action support.

06

Training

Hands-on training mechanically, AAR Field Manual Training, Billing, Inspections, and Gauging for your team.
